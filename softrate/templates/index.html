What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a unified, multi-stage, destination-based indirect tax that replaced numerous federal and state taxes like VAT, Service Tax, and Excise Duty in India.

CGST vs SGST vs IGST

  • CGST & SGST: Levied on Intra-State transactions (within the same state). The tax collected is shared equally between the Central Government (CGST) and State Government (SGST).
  • IGST: Levied on Inter-State transactions (between two different states). Collected by the Central Government and apportioned to the destination state.

Frequently Asked Questions

GST Exclusive: The amount entered represents the raw price of the product or service before taxes. The GST calculation adds the tax amount on top of this base amount.

GST Inclusive: The amount entered represents the final price paid, which already includes the tax. The calculator extracts the base price and the tax component from this total.

For transactions occurring within a single state (Intra-state), the total GST rate is divided equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For example, a transaction subject to an 18% GST slab will be split into 9% CGST and 9% SGST.

Integrated GST (IGST) applies when the location of the supplier and the place of supply are in different states (Inter-state supply). It also applies on import of goods or services into India.

GST rate structures and adjustments are governed by the GST Council, a joint forum of the Center and States chaired by the Union Finance Minister of India.
