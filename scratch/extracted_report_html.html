A financial report, also called a financial statement, is a formal record that depicts the financial position of a business at any given point in time. There are three vital financial reports that every business must generate to gauge its financial performance over the fiscal year.

The balance sheet

The balance sheet is a key financial statement that provides details about a company's assets, liabilities and shareholder's equity at any point in time. By comparing two or more balance sheets from different points in time, a company can know how much it has grown, making the balance sheet an excellent indicator of business growth.

The income statement

The income statement is also called the profit and loss statement, statement of operation, statement of financial result or income, or earnings statement. It is a picture of a company's income and expenditure. The income statement indicates whether a company has made a profit or loss for a given period.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is a financial report?

    A financial report is a formal record of the financial activities and position of a business, person, or other entity. It provides a structured summary of financial performance over a specific period, typically including statements like the Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss (Income Statement), and Cash Flow statement.

  • What are the key components of a financial report?

    The primary components include the Balance Sheet (showing assets, liabilities, and equity), the Income Statement (showing revenue, cost of goods sold, expenses, and net profit), and the Cash Flow Statement (tracking cash inflows and outflows from operating, investing, and financing activities).

  • What is the difference between a Balance Sheet and a Profit & Loss Statement?

    A Balance Sheet represents a snapshot of a company's financial position at a single point in time (what it owns and owes), while a Profit & Loss (P&L) statement measures the company's financial performance (revenues, costs, and expenses) over a specific duration of time.

  • Why are financial reports important for my business?

    Financial reports help business owners, investors, and lenders evaluate profitability, manage cash flow, secure funding, analyze operational trends, and ensure tax compliance.
