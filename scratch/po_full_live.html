Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Purchase Order?

A purchase order is a financial document that you (buyer) issue to your vendor (seller) when you are in shortage of supplies and need to restock them. It contains the details regarding the products or services that you want to buy, along with the quantity and pricing information. After the vendor receives the purchase order, it might undergo negotiations. The finalized purchase order becomes binding on the vendor to supply the products or services. 

How is an online Purchase Order Generator useful to me?

With Zoho's online Purchase Order Generator, you can easily create professional purchase orders online and print them without having to sign up or purchase any application.

How do I create a purchase order using the Purchase Order Generator?

Go to the online Purchase Order Generator.


All fields in the purchase order are editable. Click each placeholder to fill up your own details in its place. 


Click Print Purchase Order or Get PDF in the right sidebar to either quickly print it or download it to your system as a PDF file, for free.

Can I generate purchase orders with other templates?

You can sign up for Zoho Inventory to try the various predefined templates and customize them to suit your preference, for free. To do so:

  • Click the Gear icon at the top-right corner of the app.
  • Go to Templates > Purchase Orders.
  • Click + New under the Purchase Order Templates page.
  • Hover your cursor over the template of your choice and click Use This.
