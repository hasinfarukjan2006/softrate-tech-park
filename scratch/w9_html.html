KNOW MORE ABOUT W-9 FORM

What is W-9?

Form W-9 is an IRS form used to verify a contractor's identity and gather their Tax Identification Number (TIN).

Who needs a W-9 Form?

If you're hiring freelancers or contractors, or making interest payments, you'll need a Form W-9 from the payee. This information is commonly used for Form 1099 reporting.

How do I fill out a W-9 form?

Fill out the legal name, business name (if applicable), TIN, business type, address, certification options, and confirm the declarations before generating the form.
