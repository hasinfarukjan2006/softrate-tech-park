What is wholesale price?

The wholesale price is the amount a retailer pays to a supplier for a product before adding their own margin.

What you need to know?

Consider all direct costs, overhead, and desired profit when setting your wholesale price.

How wholesale price is calculated?

We add all costs, multiply by the number of units, then apply the profit margin.

Formula

Total Cost = (Cost per unit + Overhead + Administrative) × Units
Wholesale Price = Total Cost × (1 + ProfitMargin/100)

Wholesale price vs Retail price

Retail price includes the retailer's margin on top of the wholesale price.
